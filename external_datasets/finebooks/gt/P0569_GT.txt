THE SMOOTH BULIMUS. Shell somewhat fusiformly ovate,
umbilicated, thick, solid, slightly contracted at the
base, whorls ﬁve to six in number, longitudinally
striated in a wrinkled manner, columella plaited, lip
thickened, reﬂected; whitish, clouded and variegated
with greyish-red streaks.

GMELIN, Syst. Nat. p. 3436.

Cochlogena auris-caprinus, De Férussac.
Auricula Sileni, Lamarck.
Bulimus œgotis, Menke.

THE DISTORTED BULIMUS. Shell fusiformly oblong,
thick, solid, angularly compressed at the base, whorls
ﬁve to six in number, longitudinally striated in a
wrinkled manner, sometimes crenulated at the sutures,
columella strongly plaited, aperture triangularly con‐
tracted, lip thickened, reﬂected; whitish, variegated
and partially streaked with ash-red, columella deep
purple.

BRUGUIERE, Enc. Méth. vol. i. p. 344.

Auris Midœ distortus, Chemnitz.
Cochlogena distorta, De Férussac.
Plekocheilus distortus, Beck.

Hab. Venezuela.

This is the type of the group, Sp. 355 to 358, which, as
before mentioned, I am inclined to think are but one
species in diﬀerent states.

Species 359. (Mus. Cuming.)

BULIMUS RIMATUS. Bul. testâ cylindraceo-elongatâ, tenui,
subcorneâ, latè umbilicatâ, anfractibus sex, lœvibus,
nitidiusculis, columellâ expansâ, aperturâ suborbiculari

THE CLEFTED BULIMUS. Shell cylindrically elongated,
thin, somewhat horny, broadly umbilicated, whorls
six in number, smooth, rather shining, columella ex‐
panded, aperture nearly orbicular and produced; light
brown.

PFEIFFER, Pro. Zool. Soc., 1846. p. 112.

Hab.—?

Mainly distinguished by the rounded Pupa-like growth
of the aperture.

lineolis ruﬁs hic illic notatâ.

THE LOFTY‐DWELLING BULIMUS. Shell oblong-ovate,
rather thick, scarcely umbilicated, whorls seven in
number, very minutely ﬂatly granulated, longitudinally
rudely elevately striated, columella reﬂected, some‐
what twisted, lip simple, slightly expanded; whitish,
stained with pale brownish ash-colour towards the
apex, and marked here and there with longitudinal
red lines.

For authority, locality, and remarks on this species see
PI. XVII. Sp. 98, in which place a shell is represented
agreeing better with the B. lithoicus of D’Orbigny.

SCHIEDE’S BULIMUS. Shell ovate, rather largely um‐
bilicated, thick, whorls six in number, convex, lon‐
gitudinally roughly striated, columella reﬂected,
thickened, lip thickened, not reﬂected; chalk-white,

PFEIFFER, Monog. Bul. p. 187.

Hab. Mexico.

A very solid opake chalk-white shell, tinged with saﬀron
orange in the interior.
