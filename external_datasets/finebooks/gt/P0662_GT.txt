THE SLIGHT BULIMUS. Shell acuminately ovate, scarcely
umbilicated, whorls six in number, longitudinally
very ﬁnely elevately striated, columella shortly
reﬂected, lip simple ; transparent white, streaked
with pale brown near the apex.

Hab. Central America.

It is possible that this may be a light variety of a species
of more colour from the indications of marking near the
apex.

THE WELL‐TRIMMED BULIMUS. Shell acuminately ovate,
