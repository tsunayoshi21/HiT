Species 3. (Mus. Cuming.)

MESALIA MELANIOIDES. Mes. testâ acuminato-turritâ,
basi rotundatâ, anfractibus decem, supernè leviter con‐

cavis, obliquè longitudinaliter plicato-costellatis, striis
spiralibus numerosis lirisque subdistantibus decussatis,
suturis impressis; albâ, punctis raris rufo-fuscis ornatâ.

THE MELANIA-LIKE MESALIA. Shell acuminately tur-
reted, rounded at the base, whorls ten in number,
slightly concave at the upper part, obliquely longitu‐
dinally plicately ribbed, decussated with numerous
spiral striæ; and distant ridges, sutures impressed;
white, ornamented with one or two red-brown dots.

Hab.—?

The generic peculiarities of Mesalia are well-illustrated
in this species.
