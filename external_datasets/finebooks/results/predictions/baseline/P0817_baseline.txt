THE OBELISK ACHATINA. Shell elongately turreted, whorls nine to ten in number, flattened, slightly concave in the middle, transversely obscurely and irregularly ridged, last whorl obtusely angled at the base, columella arched and truncated; dirty brownish-white, covered with a horny fulvous-brown epidermis, last whorl banded with chestnut-black around the columella.
