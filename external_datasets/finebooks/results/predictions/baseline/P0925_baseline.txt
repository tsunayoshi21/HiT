TURRITELLA.—PLATE IV.

BANKS' TURRITELLA. Shell pyramidally turreted, thick, rather swollen and obtusely angled at the base, whorls fifteen in number, very minutely granulously reticulated throughout, concave, encircled with a middle ridge and other irregular smaller ones, rounded at the lower edge, sutures indistinct, columella peculiarly produced in a twisted manner, aperture somewhat square; ash-olive, mottled with black and white, ridges articulated with black.

GRAY, MSS. Brit. Mus.
Hab. Panama (dredged from sandy mud at the depth of ten fathoms); Cuming.

A solid pyramidal shell in which the whorls are convex in the middle, rounded at the lower edge, and encircled with neatly articulated ridges, one of which is central and prominent.
