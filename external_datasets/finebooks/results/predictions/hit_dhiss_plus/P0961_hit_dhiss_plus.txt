Species 3. (Mus. Cuming.)

MESALIA MELANIOIDES. Mes. testá acuminato-turritá, basi rotundatá, anfractibus decem, supernè leviter convávis, obliquè longitudinaliter plicato-costellatis, striis spiralibus numerosis lirisque subdistantibus decussatis, suturis impressis; alba, punctis raris rufo-fuscis ornatá.

THE MELANIA-LIKE MESALIA. Shell acuminately turreted, rounded at the base, whorls ten in number, slightly concave at the upper part, obliquely longitudinally plicately ribbed, decussated with numerous spiral striae and distant ridges, sutures impressed; white, ornamented with one or two red-brown dots.

The generic peculiarities of Mesalia are well-illustrated in this species.
